# Prompty pre AI fotky a obrázky

Táto zbierka obsahuje prompty z dokumentu **Prompty AI.docx**, upravené do prehľadného Markdown formátu. Každý prompt je v samostatnom kódovom bloku, aby sa dal jednoducho označiť a kopírovať do ChatGPT, DALL-E, Midjourney, Stable Diffusion, Flux, Gemini alebo iného nástroja na generovanie a úpravu obrázkov.

> Poznámka: Zjavné zalomenia riadkov a technické artefakty vzniknuté pri extrakcii dokumentu boli typograficky normalizované. Význam promptov nebol menený. Identické duplicity sú uvedené iba raz.

## 1. Štúdiový portrét ženy s pastelovo ružovými vlasmi

**Popis:** Prompt vytvorí realistický módny štúdiový portrét mladej ženy s pastelovo ružovými vlasmi zapletenými do bočného vrkoča. Hodí sa na beauty fotografiu, fashion editoriál, profilové fotografie alebo vizuály s čistým bielym pozadím.

```text
High-fidelity studio portrait of a young woman with pastel pink hair in a side braid. She wears a cream polka-dot lace top under a pink knit cardigan, accessorized with layered gold necklaces. Soft rosy makeup, glossy lips, direct gaze. Pure white background, high-key diffuse lighting, hyper-realistic fashion photography, 8k resolution.
```

## 10. Luxusný mužský editoriál v stredomorskej vile

**Popis:** Rozsiahly produkčný prompt pre autentický mužský lifestyle portrét v luxusnej vile pri mori. Presne určuje identitu, telesné proporcie, kompozíciu, fotoaparát, scénu, príbeh, pózu, výraz, oblečenie, svetlo, farebnú paletu aj požadovanú mieru fotorealizmu.

```text
Ultra-realistic luxury masculine editorial portrait, authentic high-end lifestyle photography, created exactly from the provided reference image while preserving the subject's complete facial identity with absolute accuracy.

Preserve every facial feature, hairstyle, facial proportions, skin characteristics, and overall identity exactly as shown in the reference image. Never reinterpret, beautify, stylize, or modify the subject's identity in any way.

Create a naturally fit physique with refined proportions, relaxed shoulders, healthy posture, and understated masculine elegance. The body should appear lean, balanced, and naturally athletic without any exaggerated muscular definition. Never create bodybuilder proportions or oversized muscles.

Create a photograph that feels completely authentic, as though captured for the summer edition of Robb Report, GQ, or a luxury lifestyle magazine. The image must feel like a real moment rather than a fashion pose.

Aspect Ratio: 4:5

Composition

Vertical editorial composition.
Framed from approximately mid-thigh upward.
The subject occupies approximately 77% of the frame.
Captured using a Hasselblad X2D 100C medium-format camera paired with a 90mm lens at f/2.2.
The camera is positioned slightly above waist height and approximately 35 degrees to the subject's right, creating a natural three-quarter perspective unlike previous portraits.

Scene

An elegant Mediterranean-inspired luxury villa overlooking the sea.
Natural limestone arches.
Soft ivory plaster walls.
Travertine flooring.
Large linen curtains gently moving in the sea breeze.
Olive trees and the blue horizon remain softly visible in the distance.
Everything feels calm, refined and timeless.

Story

It is early evening.
The golden sunlight has softened.
He has just finished reading several pages of a notebook filled with future ideas.
Instead of continuing, he closes the notebook and simply pauses to enjoy the breeze and the silence.
The photographer captures that genuine pause without interrupting him.

Pose

The subject stands beside one of the stone arches.
His right shoulder lightly rests against the stone column without leaning heavily.
His left foot naturally crosses slightly in front of the right, creating an effortless relaxed stance.
His left hand loosely holds the closed leather notebook against his thigh.
His right hand rests naturally inside his trouser pocket.
His posture is relaxed, elegant and completely believable.
Nothing appears staged.

Head & Expression

His head turns gently toward the open sea.
His eyes remain focused on the distant horizon.
The photographer captures him in profile, revealing approximately 70% of his face.
There is no smile.
Instead, a peaceful expression of gratitude, clarity and quiet fulfilment appears naturally through his eyes and relaxed facial muscles.
The viewer should feel that he is enjoying one rare moment of complete freedom.

Wardrobe

A luxurious long-sleeve white linen shirt made from premium Italian linen.
The sleeves are casually rolled to just below the elbows.
The top two buttons remain open naturally.
Elegant tailored sand-coloured linen trousers.
Dark brown suede loafers without socks.
A minimalist stainless-steel luxury wristwatch.
No logos.
No excessive accessories.
Every linen fibre, stitch, fold and natural wrinkle must remain beautifully visible.

Lighting

Soft golden-hour sunlight enters from the open side of the villa.
Warm rim light gently outlines the hair, shoulders and shirt.
Natural reflected light from the limestone floor softly illuminates the face.
Fine highlights appear naturally within the eyes.
Shadows remain soft, dimensional and physically accurate.
No dramatic lighting.
No artificial HDR.

Photography Quality

True medium-format realism.
Natural skin pores.
Authentic beard texture.
Individual hair strands.
Visible linen fibres.
Realistic stone texture.
Natural colour science.
Creamy medium-format depth of field.
Zero CGI appearance.
Zero beauty filters.
Zero plastic skin.
Zero oversharpening.

Background

The villa architecture, flowing curtains, olive trees and distant sea gradually dissolve into luxurious creamy bokeh while maintaining realistic atmospheric depth.

Colour Palette

Warm ivory
Limestone beige
Sand
Mediterranean blue
Olive green
Soft golden sunlight
Natural skin tones
Muted luxury neutrals

The final photograph should feel like a private editorial moment rather than a luxury advertisement. The villa, the sea, the architecture and the wardrobe quietly support the story, while the true emotional focus remains the subject's calm presence. The image should invite the viewer to imagine themselves standing there, feeling the warm breeze, hearing the distant sea, and experiencing the rare peace that comes after years of purposeful work.
```
